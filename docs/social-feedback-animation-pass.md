# Social Skills feedback and motion pass

Added a shared visual feedback routine for Social Detective, Say It Better, and standard SEL question screens. Once a real answer is selected, the scene actors perform a short approach/nod or pause motion and a descriptive outcome appears in the illustrated scene. Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. Existing choice evaluation, rewards, navigation, and localStorage formats are unchanged.

This is a focused feedback enhancement, not full bespoke acting for all 18 outcomes. Verify each scene and choice separately before claiming cinematic realism.
